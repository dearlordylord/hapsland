Recommendation and limits
Prefer Forked Campus for extending preparation into variable-n review units; keep Switchback as the clearest introductory map. Junction is a useful alternative for stronger intake/output separation. This is a visual/architectural recommendation, not evidence of measured enjoyment or learning.
The current fixture still emits exactly two authored units per source observation. A unit is not itself a finding. Variable-n generation and a quality tower that reduces later findings probability are future mechanics, not implemented here. They must remain clearly labeled host/environment experiments and cannot rewrite observed PendingFinding results.
Parent-family identity is less discoverable than in the discarded edit-card view: road actors show unit IDs, without a direct parent label or connective highlight. The current frames establish fan-out motion, not that a player can independently trace every unit to its source. A future compact parent label or selection highlight should address that before claiming source-level teaching success.
Campus's SOURCE QUEUE label visibly overlaps the paused header near the upper left. Junction's retention approach has an approximately five-pixel visual gap before the right building wall. These are bounded presentation defects, not hidden state changes; processing labels, queue labels, branch counts, actors and resource totals remain readable. Small unit IDs also become crowded around a full internal loop. These limitations do not block comparison of the requested road-map direction, but should be cleaned up in the selected layout.
The current game uses global service investments in fixed docks, not spatial tower-radius tactics. Allocation is constrained by 120 gold versus costs 50/50/40. A bounded Coordinator comparison demonstrates earlier acknowledged delivery with the same authored outcomes; strategic depth, equal-spend balance and replay value remain unmeasured.
