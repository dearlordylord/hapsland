Two Shield checks use different charge-spend inputs but assert the same transient classification; they are checks, not separate universal laws or text assertions. Native execution/typecheck success is reported in validation-no-effect-native.txt; this reviewer did not independently rerun those commands. Screenshots are native Linux ARM64 1024x768 evidence, not a claim of human engagement or other-platform support. Small text can overlap existing building annotations in the invalid case; this is nonblocking because the red radius, CANNOT BUILD text and detail panel remain legible.

Teaching value
The feedback distinguishes whether placement is allowed, whether the tower covers a structurally eligible building, and what changes in the frozen state or policy. It therefore avoids teaching that idle traffic makes a placement permanently useless, or that a batch policy change guarantees immediate throughput.
