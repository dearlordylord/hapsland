SOURCE REVIEW
RoadPreview compares RoadGame.supported_config before and after an ordinary pure hypothetical G.click. It never returns that hypothetical game state to the driver. Thus displayed marginal changes include existing tower bonuses, mechanics-selected nearest eligible site, the Parallelizer two-extra-permit cap, Packager support cap and overhead floor. Derived configurations are not passed back through helpers that would apply buffs twice. Legality uses snapped placement plus the real raw-click existing-tower selection boundary; insufficient gold and terminal state suppress actionable changes.
The renderer retains current occupancy, active work, storage and committed writer statistics. Only the prospective capacity/policy delta is appended. Packager text says NEXT BATCH ITEM OVERHEAD, preserving the distinction between future policy and an already committed reservation. Number keys1–7 explicitly return from inspection to build mode; moving the mouse alone preserves inspection, while U retains the selected tower for ordinary upgrading.
Positive deltas are cyan, zero deltas amber, cost red. Legal no-target and saturated purchases retain their actual cost, preventing the false inference that no benefit means a free purchase. Dynamic processor, Coordinator and Shield effects receive an explicit no-fixed-capacity/throughput-delta explanation.

ARCHITECTURAL LESSON AND REMAINING LIMITS
The observable lesson is a controlled prediction and purchase: an extra permit changes capacity without increasing storage or proving another job will be active, while lower per-item overhead and a larger flush target change future batching policy without promising an additional member or lower latency. Existing bonuses visibly affect the next marginal purchase.
Rapid/Batch/Relay delivered work and throughput still depend on available targets, active permits, remaining effort and cooldowns. Coordinator benefit depends on an active blocker and the full sibling-readiness condition. Shield prevention depends on local occupancy, charge, overlap and the half-pressure bound. Actual Packager batch membership, flush latency, Stop outcomes and committed reservation size are not forecast. The README correctly records prospective dynamic visuals as proposals rather than implemented guarantees.
