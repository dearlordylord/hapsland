# Historical nine-rule comparison

**Purpose:** Preserve the scope and interpretation of a completed synthetic review comparison.
**Audience:** Evaluation contributors and reviewers; readers of comparative research.
**Status:** Historical summary; the study is closed.
**Authority:** Comparative research and validation evidence, not a product contract or release certification.
**Expected use:** Understand what the archived comparison measured before consulting its immutable source snapshot.
**Lifecycle:** Keep this short pointer while the comparison index links the study; remove it if that navigation is retired.

The completed comparison used two separate matrices: four duplicate-fact designs
repeated three times, and a set of two constructed defects for each of eight other
rules plus a replacement batch for the resource rule. The reported selected results
were 12/12 Hapsland repairs versus 1/12 Abide repairs on the duplicate-fact cases,
and 6/16 versus 3/16 on the broader rule matrix. These team-authored inputs do not
establish general advice quality or isolate the cause of a difference.

The [immutable report snapshot](https://github.com/dearlordylord/hapsland/blob/e0a071afea12c1808f54aefba4ff2d44bc825341/docs/abide-contextual-review-study.md)
and its [archived evidence tree](https://github.com/dearlordylord/hapsland/tree/e0a071afea12c1808f54aefba4ff2d44bc825341/evidence/abide-contextual-current)
retain the original inputs, execution records and limitations. The archived
account is not rerun by current checks.
