# Historical larger-declaration comparison

**Purpose:** Preserve the scope and interpretation of a completed synthetic review comparison.
**Audience:** Evaluation contributors and reviewers; readers of comparative research.
**Status:** Historical summary; the study is closed.
**Authority:** Comparative research and validation evidence, not a product contract or release certification.
**Expected use:** Understand what the archived comparison measured before consulting its immutable source snapshot.
**Lifecycle:** Keep this short pointer while the comparison index links the study; remove it if that navigation is retired.

The completed comparison covered six team-authored scenarios and 36 input variants.
Across twelve native defect sessions, Hapsland repaired 11 and Abide repaired 2;
the study also recorded one Hapsland false warning among 36 repeated clean
observations. These selected sessions do not show that declaration size caused a
difference or that either product is generally superior.

The [immutable report snapshot](https://github.com/dearlordylord/hapsland/blob/e0a071afea12c1808f54aefba4ff2d44bc825341/docs/abide-large-declaration-study.md)
and [archived evidence tree](https://github.com/dearlordylord/hapsland/tree/e0a071afea12c1808f54aefba4ff2d44bc825341/evidence/abide-large-declarations-current)
retain the original inputs, execution records, checks and limitations. The archived
account is not rerun by current checks.
