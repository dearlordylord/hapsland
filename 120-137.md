## Decisions spanning the slices

- **Current authority and architectural choice:** [#116](https://github.com/dearlordylord/hapsland/issues/116) targets one Bend decision authority. Production and the full-flow page now use [`Canonical.step`](packages/agent-flow-bend/Canonical.bend) through the shared checked [adapter](src/canonical/adapter.ts); the [#137 structural gate](scripts/check-production-authority.mjs) rejects direct production imports of older generated policy wrappers. `Lifecycle.apply` and `Flow.step` remain model or historical paths without production or page transition consumers. #116 originally proposed evolving `Lifecycle.apply` in place, so the separate `Canonical.step` endpoint is a consequential architectural choice for owner review. The #137 report records the resulting boundary; it does not amend #116's original wording.
- **Native fact boundary:** parsing, strict decoding, filesystem/Git observations, glob matching, clock/deadline measurements, source capture, credential retrieval, Effect/Jev requests, promises, IPC and host writes stay in TypeScript; Bend receives explicit source-free facts and chooses policy outcomes. Display, layout and instrumentation can remain TypeScript projections only while they cannot affect a decision. [#137's report](https://github.com/dearlordylord/hapsland/issues/137#issuecomment-5873005822) inventories this split, and its structural check guards the shared adapter and former independent work/round state. Boolean facts such as `currentWork`, `selected`, `credentialReady` and `extraPending` still warrant case-by-case owner scrutiny when their native definitions change: an import guard cannot prove each is only a measurement.
- **One logical transition state, retained native identity:** the canonical [state](packages/agent-flow-bend/Canonical.bend) carries numeric identities, stages, counters and ledger charges. TypeScript still maps exact runtime strings, source-bearing keys, payloads and effect handles to those numbers in [`capacity.ts`](src/resident/capacity.ts), [`composed-delivery.ts`](src/resident/composed-delivery.ts), and [`server.ts`](src/resident/server.ts). #137 removed independently advanced generated Work/Round policy state from those adapters. Older generated artifacts remain as checked historical/model outputs, without direct production policy consumers. Structural checks establish imports and known state boundaries; they do not establish every possible runtime path or race.
- **Resident accounting and display:** installed limits are **64 items/8 MiB per resident** and **16 items/2 MiB per advicee** in [`capacity.ts`](src/resident/capacity.ts). [`Ledger.bend`](packages/agent-flow-bend/Ledger.bend) defines six charged purposes—observation/dispatch, preparation, review unit, stored result, operational notice and advice recheck—and checks the same four limits for each. A charge is logical reserved space, not process heap or Jev capacity. #135's [dashboard](packages/agent-flow-viz/src/production-main.ts) now displays the inventory generated from compiled Bend output and live shared/per-advicee projections. The installed 32 KiB source cap and logical byte limits remain separate from the accepted future 256 KiB per-file and 20 KiB evidence-tree target in [#93](docs/issue-93-type-function-review-spec.md) and [#138](https://github.com/dearlordylord/hapsland/issues/138). [#139](https://github.com/dearlordylord/hapsland/issues/139) owns retry/backpressure; refusal is immediate today.
- **Validation boundary:** #120–#135 report independent source-free traces, Bend proofs, offline resident fixtures, builds, package checks and #135 browser replay. #136 then observed bounded real Codex and Claude sessions with Jev: Codex cited delivered advice and reached clear; Claude changed source after a Stop finding and received a further finding, without final acknowledgement. These are selected host observations, not a reliability estimate or exhaustive race proof. #137's final source-linked audit combines those layers and records the same-file/32 KiB installed limit. Source, secrets and source-bearing backend responses are excluded from retained evidence.

## Issue-by-issue audit

### #120 — cross-advicee capacity

**Accepted:** [#120](https://github.com/dearlordylord/hapsland/issues/120) requires one shared ledger, ordered partial replacement, exact release and a Bend-produced charge inventory/refusal reason. **Chosen:** [`Canonical.bend`](packages/agent-flow-bend/Canonical.bend) releases the preparation charge first and then tries each unit independently in source order; a refused unit does not block a later one. [`CapacityLedger`](src/resident/capacity.ts) retains object-capability identity, numeric partition mapping and an idempotent native release fence while Bend owns charge admission, purpose and usage. All six current purposes use the same global/local count and byte limits in [`Ledger.bend`](packages/agent-flow-bend/Ledger.bend). **Open:** this is a logical ledger; changing caps or adding retry/backpressure is a later product decision. The 256 KiB file target can change measured preparation/result sizes enough to force that decision. **Evidence:** [#120 completion record](https://github.com/dearlordylord/hapsland/issues/120) reports competing advicees, accepted→refused→accepted, stale/duplicate release, a fake-clock resident fixture and 581 offline tests. Main commits `e999867`, `00f542b`, `d49efd7`, merge `b90a524`; [canonical traces](conformance/canonical-v1.json) and [boundary guard](scripts/check-capacity-boundary.mjs) are the narrower executable evidence.

### #121 — permits and rounds

**Accepted:** [#121](https://github.com/dearlordylord/hapsland/issues/121) requires original tool identity/deadline, one-use permits, precise stale/wrong-scope rejection, and a fresh next round only after valid attribution. **Chosen:** native `(advicee, tool_use_id)` strings map to positive IDs at the canonical edge; the resident keeps the original deadline and retry correlation while Bend stores permit/round fences. The non-installed fixture path issues a synthetic permit through the same transition; installed hooks require a matching pre-edit permit. The separate generated round projection present at this checkpoint was removed in #137; [`composed-delivery.ts`](src/resident/composed-delivery.ts) now retains native bindings and calls the canonical adapter. **Open, material:** [child identity evidence](docs/issue-121-permit-evidence.md#child-identity-assessment) does not establish whether a fully markerless child edit/Stop callback can occur on supported hosts. A callback with child markers but no ID is rejected; a wholly markerless child could be indistinguishable from a main-agent callback. The conservative rule can also suppress a legitimate Claude `--agent` event. Obtain a real child-edit/Stop host witness before treating parent/child isolation as fully verified. **Evidence:** nine [permit traces](conformance/canonical-permits-v1.json), offline callback-order fixtures and 585 reported tests; `bb18db0`, `37aa222`, merge `cdb00d9`.

### #122 — observation fan-out and review outcomes

**Accepted:** [#122](https://github.com/dearlordylord/hapsland/issues/122) requires zero/many review units, exact operation fencing, and finding only after a completed review. **Chosen:** source capture and semantic preparation remain effects, then each unit gets a canonical operation and charge. Current finding charges change from `reviewUnit` to `storedResult`; clear/failure/stale paths release; revalidation temporarily uses `adviceRecheck`. The `currentWork` Boolean is supplied by native code at the result barrier; see [`Canonical.bend`](packages/agent-flow-bend/Canonical.bend) and [`capacity.ts`](src/resident/capacity.ts). **Open:** `StaleOperation` groups duplicate completion and late completion because canonical state does not retain terminal ID history; diagnosis needs native callback timing. No cross-file evidence graph is installed yet, and the source cap remains 32 KiB. **Evidence:** [#122 report](docs/issue-122-review-evidence.md), [13 review traces](conformance/canonical-review-v1.json), resident two-result fan-out and 587 reported offline tests; `a3e06cc`, `8874b27`, merge `b8056e2`.

### #123 — FIFO dispatch

**Accepted:** [#123](https://github.com/dearlordylord/hapsland/issues/123) calls for finite FIFO cycles, concurrency decisions, exact cancellation identities and native promises as effects. **Chosen:** [`Canonical.bend`](packages/agent-flow-bend/Canonical.bend) owns queue order, sequence and cycle facts with a **two-slot** concurrency ceiling; [`DispatchCycles`](src/resident/dispatch.ts) executes only emitted starts and keeps native job handles. Queued versus running discard and named versus full-cohort cancellation are canonical choices. **Open:** two slots and the cycle boundary are installed policy constants; confirm them against future performance and resident capacity changes rather than treating them as dictated by the issue. This slice did not settle Stop waiting; #124 did. **Evidence:** [#123 report](docs/issue-123-dispatch-evidence.md), [dispatch traces](conformance/canonical-dispatch-v1.json), gated promise/Jev order fixtures and 588 reported tests; `64453db`, `8ecdde9`, `8bb5ebb`, merge `348604c`.

### #124 — Stop wait and cutoff

**Accepted:** [#124](https://github.com/dearlordylord/hapsland/issues/124) requires the original deadline, all unfinished work, one fenced cutoff and exact cancellation IDs. **Chosen:** the Stop poll names a group and its exact edit partitions/rounds; it waits for source/review plus native permit, lease and notice owners. At cutoff Bend releases unfinished review charges, emits cancellation only for unfinished dispatch entries, and chooses readiness versus the **four-continuation** limit. A continuing round clears the fence; closure retires it. A completed finding remains charged even if its dispatch callback is still running. [`composed-delivery.ts`](src/resident/composed-delivery.ts) retains native wait/Stop bindings; #137 removed its independently advanced Round policy state. **Open:** native `extraPending` facts deserve an authority audit when their derivation changes, and the four-continuation cap is a product value to confirm. **Evidence:** [#124 report](docs/issue-124-stop-evidence.md), [18 Stop traces](conformance/canonical-stop-v1.json), held `afterAdvicePending` regression and 589 reported offline tests; `e2d63d2` through `09f4fbb`, merge `a4f9832`.

### #125 — collection and leases

**Accepted:** [#125](https://github.com/dearlordylord/hapsland/issues/125) requires ordered fitting advice, exclusive leases, background claims and native revalidation facts. **Chosen:** [`CollectionState.bend`](packages/agent-flow-bend/CollectionState.bend) and [`Canonical.bend`](packages/agent-flow-bend/Canonical.bend) keep numeric owners, readiness, expiry, fit and claim state. The resident reserves a lease before asynchronous revalidation, then releases on invalid facts or failure before authorization; elapsed time and encoded host bytes arrive from TypeScript. Ordinary and background collectors use the same canonical state. **Open:** a transient revalidation failure retains eligible advice, so its retry opportunity depends on future collection/expiry timing; confirm this with any revised retention policy. Stop reoffer was deliberately assigned to #126. **Evidence:** [#125 report](docs/issue-125-collection-evidence.md), [21 collection traces](conformance/canonical-collection-v1.json), overlapping collector/lease fixtures and 590 reported tests; `e02e31c`, `11c7244`, `f0817a3`, merge `ad3bd94`.

### #126 — delivery and acknowledgement

**Accepted:** [#126](https://github.com/dearlordylord/hapsland/issues/126) requires one authorized handoff, a continuation reserved before output, selected-unit revalidation, truthful terminal results and one same-round Stop reoffer. **Chosen:** [`Canonical.bend`](packages/agent-flow-bend/Canonical.bend) and [`SubmissionState.bend`](packages/agent-flow-bend/SubmissionState.bend) retain the Stop slot, selected work units, batch fingerprints, writer phase and terminal state. A known unwritten provisional offer can roll back; an acknowledged or uncertain offer cannot be retried as a fresh Stop write. An acknowledged background advice submission can be reoffered at Stop once after fresh validation, without a second Jev review. [`composed-delivery.ts`](src/resident/composed-delivery.ts) retains native payload routing and performs final exact-batch checks. **Open:** “submitted” proves a host handoff, not agent visibility or repair; uncertain submission means even less. The one-reoffer rule and four-continuation budget should remain explicit product decisions. **Evidence:** [#126 report](docs/issue-126-delivery-evidence.md), [delivery](conformance/canonical-delivery-v1.json) and [submission](conformance/canonical-submission-v1.json) traces, resident expiry/ack order fixtures and 591 reported tests; `b82e4e8`, `b1d2e07`, `76620b6`, merge `aaa039c`.

### #127 — revision supersession

**Accepted:** [#127](https://github.com/dearlordylord/hapsland/issues/127) supersedes only older work for the same semantic subject and must fence late callbacks without releasing replacement capacity. **Chosen:** [`RevisionState.bend`](packages/agent-flow-bend/RevisionState.bend) stores numeric subject/input identity, generation and same-input live member count. Identical input reuses a generation; changed input replaces only that subject. Native keys are monotonic within a resident lifetime and pruned when no active revision refers to them; a stale query with a pruned key receives a fresh nonmatching ID. See [`server.ts`](src/resident/server.ts) and [#127 report](docs/issue-127-revision-evidence.md). **Open:** the exact semantic subject and complete canonical-input identity are formed natively; cross-file #93 support must broaden those keys to include the full evidence tree and review-input contract before cache/revision reuse can be claimed for imported evidence. **Evidence:** [30 revision traces](conformance/canonical-revision-v1.json), held old Jev callback/revalidation fixtures and 591 reported tests; `52543e8`, merge `deb41b6`.

### #128 — ticket settlement

**Accepted:** [#128](https://github.com/dearlordylord/hapsland/issues/128) requires truthful terminal statuses and no double settlement or revival. **Chosen:** [`TicketState.bend`](packages/agent-flow-bend/TicketState.bend) stores ordered unit phases, first retained failure reason, delivery mark and ticket phase. Final status also consumes native credential, expiry, live-advice and pending-notice facts. A finding becomes `delivered` only after acknowledged host submission is finalized; failed/uncertain sends do not imply clear. ### #129 — evaluation reuse and cache

**Accepted:** [#129](https://github.com/dearlordylord/hapsland/issues/129) requires eligible joins or successful cached reuse, exact credential/input identity and bounded eviction. **Chosen:** [`ReuseState.bend`](packages/agent-flow-bend/ReuseState.bend) routes in this priority: live advice, attached evaluation, bare claim, successful cache, new owner. Cache entries are oldest-first; hits move to newest, so eviction behaves as LRU. Commit occurs only after native `storedResult` capacity reservation and verifies its charge. Native keys include partition, work cohort, credential generation and exact prepared input; failed/malformed Jev outcomes are not cached. See [`evaluation-reuse.ts`](src/resident/evaluation-reuse.ts) and [#129 report](docs/issue-129-reuse-evidence.md). **Open:** future graph evidence and provider/request formatting must enter the exact prepared input; changing the contract should invalidate prior hits. ### #130 — operational notices

**Accepted:** [#130](https://github.com/dearlordylord/hapsland/issues/130) requires coalescing, cooldown, suppression, expiry, fit and no notice-only repair continuation. **Chosen:** [`NoticeState.bend`](packages/agent-flow-bend/NoticeState.bend) retains numeric keys, suppression counts, pending/lease state and order; a new key commits only with an `operationalNotice` charge. ### #131 — retention and lifetime cleanup

**Accepted:** [#131](https://github.com/dearlordylord/hapsland/issues/131) requires source-free closure markers, exact release, pruning/eviction and no cross-advicee damage. **Chosen:** ticket eviction uses monotonically increasing admission ID, so a later phase update does not change age. At idle cleanup, native connection and payload-presence facts feed `CleanupCheck`; Bend also requires no dispatch/work/advice/lease/reuse/notice owners. Cache entries and tickets are cleared through canonical events, then `CleanupCommit` requires an empty ledger and closes the marker in the same synchronous turn as native retirement. Verify eventual cleanup if native payload-presence facts remain true indefinitely. **Evidence:** [43 retention traces](conformance/canonical-retention-v1.json), zero-byte late callback/uncertain write regressions, 592 reported tests; `45bd404`, merge `2fbe953`.

## #145 follow-up — unified Claude delivery (candidate verified offline)

**Accepted direction:** [#145](https://github.com/dearlordylord/hapsland/issues/145) keeps one installed Claude hook flow with synchronous edit, background, and Stop output opportunities. Advisory remains the default; only a current user opt-in can authorize synchronous `block-current-findings`, and project policy can narrow that choice. Each review unit needs its admission provenance, while collection batches can span several admissions in the exact advicee and Hapsland round. A host write proves submission, not model visibility or repair.

**Assumptions requiring owner review:** A ready finding or operational notice from an earlier edit in the same round may be selected during a later edit's synchronous opportunity, provided the original admission and current authority are valid. The five-finding/2 KiB limit applies to each final serialized Claude response, including wrapper text and notices. Mixed successful findings and failed units should preserve both facts in diagnostics while still offering the finding. Two source-free identities coexist: a canonical observation ID for work provenance and an admission capability generation for collection ownership. The [#136 native evidence](docs/issue-136-native-evidence.md) supports preserving an immediate feedback opportunity, but it does not establish a general repair rate or prove advisory output ineffective.

**Implementation status:** The installed `PostToolUse` hook polls after admission and uses the same resident advice, lease, currentness, notice, and round authority as background and Stop. An unmarked direct Claude invocation returns quietly. Each review unit names its canonical admission; Bend and the resident retain flat unit facts without a reverse list or ticket-wide terminal state.
