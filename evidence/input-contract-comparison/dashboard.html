How to read the conclusion

Review guide

What passed

The candidate was operationally healthy: calls completed, repetitions were available, extraction stayed under the timing budget, and rendered requests were smaller than whole-file requests.

What failed

On the same fixtures, bounded context did not produce enough paired wins over whole-file input. The experiment cannot claim a material semantic improvement.

What follows

Keep the production contract conservative. A future narrow experiment would need a new hypothesis and explicit authorization; this issue does not authorize an extractor.
